# Addendum — completed results and statistical reanalysis (2026-11-06)

The original `research_report.md` and `execution_status.json` described a blocked inference attempt. Those files are retained as historical records. The branch now contains completed local inference artifacts for all three pinned embedding models (commit `860cc32`), including normalized embeddings, raw and centered cosine matrices, plots, result JSONs, model revisions, and hashes under `results/`.

A later audit found that the original within-language summaries used 1,050 same-domain pairs per category/language while cross-language means used 22,350 distinct-concept pairs; the cross-language bootstrap intervals incorrectly summarized within-domain subsets. The independent matrix-only repair is at `research/cooperative_lexical_geometry_v1_reanalysis/`. It preserves all original files and provides estimand-matched intervals and repeated-kernel sensitivity results.

Corrected results do not support excess cooperation coherence after per-language centering: all three model intervals for cooperation versus the mean controls include zero, both within language and across languages. Raw-cosine excess remains descriptive and has stronger permutation support for BGE-M3 and LaBSE, but the lexical materials remain templated and the translations unreviewed. Cooperation-specific geometry is still unestablished.
