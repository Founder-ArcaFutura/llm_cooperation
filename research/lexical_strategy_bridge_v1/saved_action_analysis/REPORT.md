# Saved-action semantic analysis (exploratory, 2026-11-06)

## Finding

The lexical profiles carry substantial information about the original four strategy labels, and their geometry predicts held-out variation in the saved action-prompt activations beyond a word/character-length baseline. This is consistent with meaningful shared semantic structure; it does not establish that this structure is spurious or that any model selected a strategy. Several cooperation-score comparisons are negative. All analyses reuse an already inspected corpus and are exploratory.

## Materials and integrity

The 496 exact `action_text` strings in `actions.jsonl` are the original 124 scenarios times four actions. All three activation inventories match these records, and every activation file matches its historical evidence index; the index matches its manifest either as local bytes or canonical LF bytes. The historical strict loader rejects the current code fingerprint; this independent reader checks the input evidence but does not establish that historical software was reproduced. Activations reflect the entire scenario and action prompt, at the final decision position, not isolated action embeddings.

Each saved reference array has 1,000 finite unit vectors; its raw and language-centered cosine arrays were independently recomputed and verified. The lexicon's canonical LF hash matches the freeze, and summaries identify the local CRLF bytes. E5, BGE-M3 and LaBSE were loaded from cached pinned revisions, on CPU, with the original normalized sentence-transformer preprocessing and E5 `query: ` prefix. Fourteen reference rows spanning languages and the inventory were re-encoded; maximum absolute discrepancies were 6.71e-8, 0, and 4.84e-8 respectively. This supports, but does not independently prove, the identities of all 1,000 saved rows: the original producer did not save row IDs. LaBSE's saved revision is the short `b7f9471`. The expanded corpus has not received native-fluent independent review.

`reference_rows.jsonl` records the inferred concept-major, en/fr/zh/ja/ar order, including IDs, words, language and category. Each `{encoder}_profiles.npy` is 496 by 1,000 cosine similarities, aligned with `actions.jsonl` and `reference_rows.jsonl`. Each `{encoder}_scenario_summary.jsonl` gives every action's top 10 concepts (five-language mean and individual language similarities), four category means, and within-scenario rank on each category. The `neutral` lexical category is never assigned to the DISENGAGE strategy.

## Semantic baseline

The classifier uses 200 features: for each concept, the mean action-to-word similarity across its five translations. A standardized four-class logistic classifier (C=1, balanced class weights) is fit on discovery examples only. The family and domain splits are the existing fixed partitions; both keep families intact. Each scenario has one of each action, so the evaluation action classes are balanced. Category means weight all 50 concepts per category equally. This is a supervised semantic-only baseline, not a zero-shot mapping from lexical category names to strategies. It does not use model activations. Accuracy is action-weighted; bootstrap intervals below instead use the mean of held-out group accuracies, resampling families or domains, conditional on the trained classifier.

| Encoder | Family action accuracy | Family cluster mean [95% interval] | Domain action accuracy | Domain cluster mean [95% interval] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| E5 | .860 | .829 [.769, .884] | .911 | .870 [.785, .944] |
| BGE-M3 | .864 | .870 [.815, .926] | .899 | .893 [.834, .949] |
| LaBSE | .909 | .889 [.824, .944] | .911 | .943 [.897, .983] |

Chance for balanced four-class actions is .25. Word/character length alone scored .568 family and .548 domain; adding it to the semantic features scored .871–.917 and .911–.919 respectively. The four-category-only baseline scored .705–.742 family and .673–.714 domain. Single-language concept features scored .841–.932 family and .827–.948 domain, showing language sensitivity rather than proving language invariance. Greedy removal of reference concepts with cosine at least .95 against an earlier kept mean reference leaves 146 E5, 200 BGE and 198 LaBSE concepts; held-out accuracy remains .852–.909 family and .899–.911 domain. This is only one order- and threshold-dependent redundancy sensitivity check.

Family-held-out COMPETE_EXPLOIT recall is .576, .576 and .697 for E5, BGE and LaBSE respectively, despite their higher overall accuracy. Per-strategy metrics, all language views, predictions and cluster intervals are in `results.json`; prediction arrays follow the held-out rows of `actions.jsonl` in order. Intervals do not include model selection, corpus construction, or shared training-data uncertainty.

## Geometry and probe comparison

For each encoder, model, depth and split, subtract each scenario's four-action mean separately from semantic profiles and activation vectors. Compute within-view cosine-distance matrices and Spearman RSA on held-out action pairs. No vectors from different model hidden spaces are directly compared. A discovery-pair ridge regression predicts activation distance from absolute word- and character-count differences, with and without semantic distance; report held-out pair MSE reduction relative to the length-only model. All pairs are dependent, so these are descriptive effect sizes, not independent observations or significance tests. Scenario centering uses all four candidate actions from a test scenario, making this a within-scenario comparative analysis.

Across the 3 encoders, 3 models and 8 depths, held-out RSA ranges .288–.582 for family and .287–.595 for domain. Adding semantic distance reduces held-out distance-prediction MSE by 9.7–33.6% for family and 9.6–35.7% for domain, relative to this particular length baseline. The complete 144 combinations and negative as well as positive per-strategy results are in `results.json`. These relative MSE reductions are not proportions of total neural variance explained, and neither RSA nor these models isolates semantics from correlated task/strategy structure.

Historical reports save aggregate probe metrics, per-action evaluation predictions, and class-mean strategy directions, but not per-action decision scores or fitted logistic weights. The saved raw directions have now been independently reconstructed from the saved activations and verified at every depth and split. The per-action agreement with historical predictions is in `historical_comparison.json`. Their final-depth cluster-macro balanced accuracies were Qwen 1.000/.990, OLMo .977/.974 and Mistral .991/.989 for family/domain. Their evaluation predictions are compared with the semantic classifier on the same actions in `historical_comparison.json`. As a clearly separate approximation, the present analysis refits a scenario-centered four-class logistic probe (fixed C=.1) on each discovery split and predicts held-out actions. The final-depth action accuracies are Qwen 1.000/.992, OLMo .977/.968 and Mistral .992/.988. These refits are not reproductions of the historical grouped-C-selection procedure. Per-action agreement of semantic and refitted probe predictions appears at every depth in `results.json`.

The refitted four-class probe's four decision scores are predicted from semantic profiles using a discovery-fitted ridge model, and compared on held-out actions against a discovery-fitted word/character-length ridge. Across all scores the semantic model improves MSE in these runs. Per-strategy improvements are heterogeneous: for COOPERATE, family-held-out final-block improvement is negative for Qwen E5 (-.038) and BGE (-.037), and for OLMo E5 (-.102) and BGE (-.034); positive examples and all four classes are also retained in the JSON. Values represent fractional reduction of error relative to length, not conventional variance explained. These are reconstructed multiclass score directions, not the original saved one-vs-rest fitted directions; the historical class-mean directions are separately available for a properly qualified lexical projection; logistic-coefficient transfer remains unavailable without reconstructing and validating the historical fit.

## Scope and next step

This analysis answers whether existing action-text semantic profiles covary with saved full-prompt geometry, under two existing holdouts. It cannot determine how isolated lexical items are represented inside Qwen, OLMo or Mistral, distinguish effects of the shared prompt from action text, demonstrate cross-model causal mechanisms, or recover unsaved original per-action probe outputs. New three-model lexical activation capture would answer the distinct question of within-model geometry and probe projection for isolated words versus the controlled bracket context. It should be considered only after reviewing the provisional lexical corpus, defining centering and grouped inference, and assessing these saved-data findings; this report does not authorize or recommend launching it now.

## Subsequent correction and probe recovery

The historical raw logistic probes have since been refitted with the saved selected regularization values. All historical held-out predictions match for every model, split and depth. The resulting coefficients and provenance are in `reconstructed_original_probes/`; equality of predictions does not establish identical original coefficients. `historical_comparison.json` contains direct semantic-versus-historical prediction agreement, which at the final depth ranges from .852 to .917 for the family split and .871 to .927 for the domain split.
