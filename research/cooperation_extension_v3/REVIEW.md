# Extension v3: focused correction for review

Status: development candidate, not semantically validated or ready for full inference. No new inference was launched. v1/v2 artifacts and historical results are untouched.

The bank has 90 cases and 2,592 presentations per model. Actor-benefit, counterpart-benefit and actor-time conditions each have a binary base, a three-action condition, target removal and neutral-replacement condition. Each is crossed with both decision perspectives, both identities for each physical agent, and all option permutations. Removal and replacement keep the same physical facts and the shared alternative texts/outcomes as the three-action base. They explicitly say only listed actions are available, because the common context describes actions that may be absent from a given set.

The third option now requests an independent audit of Morgan's instrument: it costs Morgan 10 minutes and yields Morgan an additional check. It therefore supplies a potentially meaningful actor-benefit alternative, but its practical plausibility and attraction require independent review. The replacement archives a duplicate with no additional check; it may be too similar to keeping the record. The third option is not one of the historical four strategies, and v3 does not reproduce that four-strategy instrument. Binary-versus-three-action presentations differ in explanatory text as well as the choice set.

Representative full rendered prompts are in REPRESENTATIVE_PROMPTS.md. Manual inspection shows that the sender-only benefit still depends on an artificial automated routing configuration, zero-minute handling is asserted, and the water and laboratory domains share a common grammar. These are development stimuli, not independent family replications. They require independent assessment of actual task mechanics, check value, feasibility, authority, identity capabilities, effort, framing, and outcome ledgers. Do not treat their asserted benefits as validated.

The v3 analyzer reports complete-order action probabilities, matched perspective and benefit/cost contrasts, perspective interactions, return/goal/harm/choice-set block contrasts, return-by-cost differences in differences, and matched cross-model block and perspective differences. Choice-set comparisons use actions present in both sets; they cannot infer an unobserved preference for the removed option. Report set-specific probabilities because normalization changes. Categorical goal/harm contrasts are packages and cannot isolate goal alignment or risk independently of outcomes. All effects remain descriptive until suitable independent family sampling and review.

Position diagnostics report letter-A dominance, per-condition winner stability across every permutation, full action-ranking stability including ties, and target-probability range. These are diagnostics, not a comprehensive validity proof. Prior v1 Mistral binary unique-max selections were A in 340/340 cases. Do not assume three-action measurements fix that problem.

The predeclared v3 diagnostic uses all water-domain benefit/cost binary and three-action conditions, identities, perspectives and orders (512 presentations per model). It can be resumed under the same canonical model run ID to fill the full bank without repeating completed diagnostic records. Only after semantic review and explicit approval should the diagnostic be launched; assess position stability and comprehension before deciding on full evaluation. GPU execution of this adapter is untested.

Structural tests verify factorial and choice-set coverage, factor/ledger consistency, option mapping, perspective contrasts, incomplete pair handling, normalized synthetic probabilities, return contrasts, and position ranking. They do not prove semantic validity. The currently proposed two domains cannot support population-level inference. A genuinely independently authored, unexposed family is required for that claim.
