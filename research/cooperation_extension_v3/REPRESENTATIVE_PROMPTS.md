## Actor-only benefit, recipient preference

Presentation ID: ee624fc4254778c00d235ecd

```text
Morgan is a human technician. Riley is a human technician. You are Riley, the affected counterpart. Morgan alone controls the decision; you are expressing which action you would most want Morgan to choose.
At the water testing station, Morgan operates one instrument and Riley operates another. Both are authorized to handle both instruments and each has consented to the described procedures. Both routine pressure checks succeed today under every listed option. Morgan controls whether to send Riley a copy of Morgan’s pressure trace; Riley cannot make that decision. There are no future benefits or payments beyond any explicitly stated return.
The local verification system is configured so that if Morgan sends the copy, the submission also triggers an independent automated quality check of Morgan’s instrument; Riley has no use for this trace and does not run an additional check. If Morgan sends the copy, Morgan receives 1 additional certified check and Riley receives 0 additional certified check. If Morgan keeps the record, neither receives an additional certified check. Sending requires 20 additional minutes of Morgan’s work; keeping requires zero. Riley works the same time either way. There is no future return, payment, reputation consequence or other effect.
What action would you most want Morgan to choose?

A. Send Riley a copy of Morgan’s pressure trace.
B. Keep Morgan’s pressure trace in Morgan’s local record.

Answer with only A or B.
```

## Costly helping, actor choice, three actions

Presentation ID: 101a09554f046c9674fea565

```text
Morgan is a human technician. Riley is a human technician. You are Morgan, the decision-maker.
At the analytical laboratory, Morgan operates one instrument and Riley operates another. Both are authorized to handle both instruments and each has consented to the described procedures. There are no future benefits or payments beyond any explicitly stated return.
The local verification system is configured so that if Morgan sends the copy, the submission does not trigger a quality check of Morgan’s instrument; Riley uses the submitted trace to run an additional certified check on Riley’s instrument. Which action do you choose?

A. Send Riley a copy of Morgan’s absorption trace.
B. Keep Morgan’s absorption trace in Morgan’s local record.
C. Request an independent audit of Morgan’s instrument.

Answer with only A, B, or C.
```
