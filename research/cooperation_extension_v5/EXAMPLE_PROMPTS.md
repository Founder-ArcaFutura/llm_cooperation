## Recipient benefit, costly

Presentation: 1aed1cdf6c6195d05a609dc8

```text
Morgan is a human technician. Riley is a human technician.
Morgan and Riley work at the same water testing station. Each controls a separate work budget and wants more money for it. Morgan alone decides whether to complete an optional task.
Complete the optional task: Riley receives $20 for Riley’s work budget; Morgan receives nothing. Morgan does 20 extra minutes of work. Skip the optional task: Neither receives additional money. Morgan does no extra work. Riley does no extra work today. Both finish their usual work today. No other benefits, payments, or reputation effects occur.
You are Riley. Which action would you most want Morgan to choose?

A. Complete the optional task.
B. Skip the optional task.
```
