# Independent first-pass review

Distribute this guide with `review_packets_blind.json` only. Do not supply the design report, generator, source comparison keys or model choices before independent ratings are frozen.

Read each packet as a standalone choice. Identify who decides, who can act, and whether all options are physically and institutionally feasible. Multiple interaction mechanisms may apply; do not force a single label.

For every displayed option, record the actor's work minutes today, future time savings, each party's routine success and additional-check success. Separate explicitly stated facts from inference or ambiguity. Say whether both contributions are necessary for a specific named outcome, and whose outcome it is. Distinguish no return from silence about return. Do not infer global utility from minutes alone.

Record plausibility, lexical favoritism, contradictions, unstated consequences, capability problems and any option that is hard to interpret. Explain each proposed exclusion. Treat an unanswered critical question as unresolved rather than guessing the author's intended level.

Review independently before discussing packets. Keep completed ratings in a separate versioned file; regeneration overwrites the blank templates. A second-stage coordinator will arrange paired comparison and source review after initial judgments are frozen. Access to private repository files containing intent or keys compromises first-pass blinding and should be recorded as exposure.
