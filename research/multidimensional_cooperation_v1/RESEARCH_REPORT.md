# Multidimensional cooperation assay: local investigation and expansion proposal

**Status:** exploratory research design and descriptive reanalysis; not a prospective replication, validated construct census, or manuscript revision. No new paid evaluations. The original banks, prior annotations, behavioral rows and manuscript are unchanged. Model-derived labels and model choices are different data. Construct label error is a live problem, as documented in `reports/manuscript_readiness/construct_luna_qa/forensic_20261113/METHODOLOGICAL_AUDIT.md`.

## A. What is in the existing evidence?

The three analyzed banks contain 152 bank-specific templates: Phase 0 has 60, Phase 0B 62 and Phase 1 30. Their human/AI counterpart versions total 304 scenarios, with original and rewritten focal-option wordings giving 608 scenario-wording combinations. `scenario_inventory.csv` records source IDs, partner, authored context, condition, triad, control and split. Four option rotations and repeated partners/wordings are presentations of related cases, not new independent scenarios. Template IDs can repeat across banks, so identify them by `(bank, template_id)`.

The 11 responsive base checkpoints have complete canonical four-option behavioral records, hash-checked against the cohort provenance manifest. Pythia's position-A pathology excludes it from this responsive cohort; instruction-tuned and exploratory fine-tuned checkpoints are not silently combined with base models. Several checkpoints share model families and pretraining histories. The historical Phase 1 `confirmatory` / held-out metadata conflicts with known runs and review chronology: source labels are preserved as provenance, not proof of prospective independent evaluation.

The [versioned taxonomy](taxonomy_v1.json) separates mechanisms, goal scope, contributions required for a named outcome, incremental outcome by party and dimension, actor costs, reciprocity, authority, harmful effects and framing. These are operational descriptors, not a proven set of independent latent traits. `annotation_schema_v1.json` permits multiple judgments, quotation provenance, unresolved and conflicting evidence, and separate source versus author metadata. A shared task need not be helpful; a useful transfer need not involve shared work; a potential cost is not a realized loss; silence is not zero. An answer about which action a recipient wants does not establish that recipient's own cooperative action.

**Coverage is preliminary.** `provisional_interaction_coverage.csv` reports Qwen's unvalidated, overlapping assignments by bank and wording. For example, Qwen assigns information sharing to 192 of 608 scenario-wording records and unilateral assistance to 54; these are not 192 and 54 independently validated scenarios. Interaction counts can exceed the number of items. By bank, Qwen assigns unilateral assistance to 54 Phase 0 wordings and none in Phase 0B or Phase 1, while exchange appears in 10 Phase 0 and 10 Phase 0B wordings and none in Phase 1. These are assignment patterns needing review, not evidence that true assistance or reciprocity is absent from the other banks. Qwen's interdependence, outcome, and cost judgments show material errors; the Luna pilot used different instructions and is not ground truth. The current annotations support exploratory screening, not a reliable measurement-based prevalence estimate.

## B. What behavioral patterns can we inspect now?

The outcome is a restricted next-token forced-choice selection, not a free interaction or evidence of internal motive. The authored `COOPERATE` option aggregates heterogeneous actions. `provisional_subtype_behavior.csv` joins Qwen's interaction labels to original behavior per model, bank and wording, including per-group template and presentation counts. It is provided for audit, not for claims about validated subtypes. A template can contribute to multiple categories and variants of one template may be assigned differently. Missing or differently perceived outcomes cannot be repaired by a statistical model.

`development_annotation_sensitivity.csv` repeats this descriptive grouping using Qwen or Luna interaction labels on the **same small development sample**, excluding invalid Luna answers. For Qwen 2.5 3B in Phase 0B original wording, the Qwen-assigned `joint_task` group includes two templates/eight presentations, while the Luna-assigned group includes four templates/28 presentations; both happen to have a +100-point COOPERATE-minus-INDEPENDENT margin. This example demonstrates that **group membership changes**; it does not demonstrate the groups have equal behavior in a population. The development sample is selected, contains repeated variants, and Luna is not a gold standard. No category-conditioned interpretation should be treated as robust merely because two such group averages happen to match.

### Authored within-family cost triads

Phase 0B contains four authored triads that vary conditions called advantageous, equal utility, and disadvantageous: water-main telemetry (01), conveyor loading (02), encryption pipeline (03), and aquifer pumping (04). We matched partner identity, option-position permutation, and wording across all three conditions; rows are included only when **both wordings in each condition** have unique forced-choice maxima. Letter-to-strategy mappings were checked for equality. `within_triad_choice_rates.csv` reports model/triad/wording results, matched presentation count, and COOPERATE and INDEPENDENT rates separately. Complete strata per model/wording are 3–4 triads; two checkpoints have no complete matched rows in triad 03. Some retained cells have only one or two matched presentations, so estimates are highly granular and selection by tie exclusion may matter.

Among the 42 available model-by-triad comparisons with original wording, the COOPERATE selection rate in the authored disadvantageous condition is lower than in the advantageous condition in 29 and equal in 13; with rewritten wording it is lower in 22 and equal in 20. These are overlapping repeated checkpoints across four families of scenarios, not 42 independent replications or a statistical test. In triad 01, OLMo 7B goes from 100% to 0% across eight matched presentations per condition, whereas Qwen 2.5 7B stays at 100% in that cell. These illustrate differing descriptive patterns, not established stable signatures.

The triplets vary wording and outcome descriptions along with the nominal utility condition. Some disadvantaged actions explicitly say “despite” a delay. The apparent cost association may reflect cost, lexical framing, consequence salience, order effects remaining after rotation, or a combination. The analysis excludes tied wording pairs; it is not a tie-inclusive sensitivity. Because there are only four triads and the strata are selectively retained, no inferential p-values or family-level generalization are justified here. The existing formal study's separate no-ties/tie-inclusive reports should be consulted rather than interpreting this as a preregistered test.

The figure `triad_selection_changes.png` shows these original-wording changes; each `n` is the number of matched presentations per condition, **not** independent situations. Missing cells had no complete retained triad match. The matched triad analysis describes scenario variation, not validated interaction subtypes.

### Alternative-set removal and wording

The earlier six-checkpoint, three-option follow-up found independence frequently selected when the authored cooperative option was removed; the existing manuscript's unique-argmax results list independence rates 43.4%–73.4%, with uneven option-letter rates and varying tied-row exclusions. This is a changed choice set, not an observed second preference from the four-option set. This investigation has not independently rejoined every three-option row; the six-checkpoint results remain as reported in `reports/manuscript_readiness/MANUSCRIPT_V1.md` and `posthoc_significance_results.json`. The existing two-wording study also found changes in margins, but the wording pairs remain semantically unadjudicated. Neither result proves a mechanism.

## C. Main confounds and evidence gaps

- The original option label is not an experimentally randomized interaction type. Some actions have joint, helping, exchange or parallel features simultaneously; their other options and contexts also change.
- Cost, partner benefit, shared infrastructure, goals, option tone and plausibility co-vary. A numerical author payoff not shown to the model is not evidence the model saw or understood that payoff.
- Qwen and Luna differed in the question, context and definition of equality, necessity and lexical appeal; the pilot is not a gold set. Existing Qwen subtype groupings should be considered an uncertainty scenario, not a complete construct census.
- The source strata are not representative samples of the space of social tasks. Few clearly specified reciprocal exchange and necessary-contribution cases are documented by current model labels, and those labels require review. Potentially important null or adverse effects are under-specified.
- Original records do not demonstrate stable preferences over repeated independent settings; they show restricted choices in authored prompts. Model differences may reflect training, format, position, wording, tie handling, task mix or other factors.

## D. Controlled extension: proposals, not ready-to-run stimuli

`candidate_families_v1.json` contains three development families with full four-option drafts and source pointers: a telemetry information-sharing time-cost contrast, a cutting-bit no-promise versus future-promise contrast, and independent versus dependent calibration. Each records the intended manipulation and known confounds. Both conditions are new drafts; none is claimed to reproduce the original anchor. Most importantly, promised return also changes expected benefit and trust, and making calibration dependent also changes the success of independent action. These are candidates for redesign and challenge, not clean isolated treatments. A structural validator checks IDs, parent templates, options and documentation; it cannot establish semantic or payoff equivalence.

`transformation_spec_v1.json` specifies parent IDs and hashes, actor/recipient authority, named outcome dimensions, constants, explicit confounds, counterfactual provenance, option order and review. Human reviewers should independently assess original and candidate source fidelity, manipulation comprehension, outcome attribution, option feasibility, identity capability, lexical tone and unintended changes. Record disagreement and uncertainty; do not force agreement or relabel originals. `candidate_blind_packets.json` and `candidate_blind_response_template.json` provide outcome- and intent-blind first-pass review materials for all six candidate conditions. `candidate_RESTRICTED_key.json` links the randomized versions, source anchors, intent and known confounds; do not expose it before first-pass review. The manipulated text is visible by necessity, and option positions are randomized once, not balanced across the proposed behavioral assay. For source fidelity, conduct a separate second review with the original anchor after first-pass independent assessments are frozen. The existing 10-packet blind review is a separate codebook pilot.

Prioritize a modest number of separately constructed, counterbalanced families across multiple domains: (1) actor cost with recipient benefit held explicitly constant where plausible, (2) information transfer versus genuine complementary joint work with realistic independent options, and (3) direct exchange versus unconditional help where future gain and risk can be described explicitly. Some contrasts cannot be perfectly orthogonal in natural stories. Document infeasible cells rather than invent scenarios, and retain symmetric and negative controls as separate historical anchors. For stronger tests, factorialize only dimensions that can be coherently manipulated, include cross-domain replication, negative controls and sham wording changes, and prespecify estimands and eligible families before inspecting new outputs.

## E–F. Evidence for or against a stable multidimensional profile

More persuasive evidence would require independently validated labels and manipulations, reproducible model-specific contrasts across multiple independently authored task families and wording sets, comparable measurement across identities and option orders, uncertainty clustered at the scenario family and model-family level, and prospective performance on genuinely unexposed cases. Predefine how many families and domains are sufficient based on the intended inferential design, with held-out families kept out of prompt development and annotation calibration. Include a direct evaluation of measurement invariance, ties, free-generation versus restricted-choice agreement and alternative-set sensitivity. Compare a simple aggregate model against a dimensional model using out-of-sample prediction on held-out scenario families, not merely in-sample fit.

The interpretation would be materially weakened if subtype rankings reverse across independent wordings or domains, disappear after equalizing framing and alternatives, fail to predict new families, are dominated by position or ties, or do not improve held-out prediction over a simpler aggregate description. Null effects, alternative-set dependence, and lack of a low-dimensional stable structure are legitimate outcomes.

## Reproduction and safety

From repository root:

```powershell
python scripts\multidimensional_cooperation_study.py
python scripts\validate_multidimensional_candidates.py
python scripts\build_multidimensional_blind_review.py
$env:PYTEST_DISABLE_PLUGIN_AUTOLOAD = '1'
python -m pytest tests/test_multidimensional_cooperation_study.py -q
```

The analysis checks hashes for packets, Qwen input and canonical behavioral rows, records hashes in `analysis_manifest.json`, and writes only this new research directory. Output files are derived artifacts; maintain copies of any manual annotations separately before regeneration. Preliminary annotation labels and new candidate texts are not scientifically approved. No API calls, new evaluation, or manuscript changes were made.
