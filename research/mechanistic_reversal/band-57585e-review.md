The 101–500 band (400 coordinates) yielded group-balanced candidate margin change -0.7467, versus -0.7391 candidate minus mean of three layer-matched random sets; the reported exploratory 95% cluster-bootstrap interval is [-0.8700, -0.6092]. All eight observed group-average changes are negative, ranging from -1.0781 to -0.4531. The 501–1,000 band alone changes the margin by +0.1139. These are real interventions on this model and measurement, but the band identities were selected based on related prior results and are not a held-out discovery of a general circuit. Bootstrap intervals condition on three random draws and do not address selection or multiple comparisons.

The 101–500 band is scattered across layers, not a single spatial location. Its 206 positively and 194 negatively correlated coordinates include 142 in layers 20–22, 163 in layers 23–25, and 95 in other layers. Correlation is calculated on 18 averaged discovery groups and does not establish a coordinate's causal sign. Candidate and random sets have matching layer counts; they are not matched by activation, output projection strength, or functional influence.

A central limitation: the mean margin change depends strongly on the displayed position of COOPERATE. Group-balanced changes are A: -0.2656, B: -1.3203, C: -1.2734, D: -0.1276. These four values are repeated measurements from the same eight groups and descriptive post-hoc strata, not independent tests. The option order changes along with each position, so this cannot be reduced to a simple letter bias. It does show that the reported average is not position invariant.

There are four tie-broken argmax changes for the target band, of which three are unique alternative winners. Two of those are human/AI variants of template 015, the third is in triad 01, hence they span two independent groups; all three use permutation p2 and switch from cooperation C to competition/exploitation A. The fourth (template 023 human p2) is an exact A/C tie and should not be described as a strict change of preference. The target yields 89/96 calibration accuracy against 90/96 baseline. This small aggregate check cannot establish general capability preservation. All calculations are based on BF16 recorded logits; post-hoc float conversion cannot recover lost precision.

The three isolated bands through rank 1,000 sum to about -0.574 compared with -0.581 for the nested 1,000 ablation in the source sweep; all five sum to about -0.430 versus -0.415 for the nested 2,000 ablation. This is approximate agreement in one aggregate mean, not a demonstration of additivity per presentation, across groups, or at neuron level. Interaction effects may cancel in the aggregate.

Proposed focused follow-up: `modal_mechanistic_focus.py` uses the exact source candidate ranking and compares the complete 101–500 band, four 100-rank partitions, positive vs negative correlation subsets, and layer groups 20–22, 23–25, and the remaining layers, each against independent layer-matched random subsets. It reports matched-random contrasts at each answer position and unique-winner/tie counts, plus calibration. These are overlapping, exploratory tests on already-inspected scenarios. Stronger controls matched by measured activation magnitude and output influence, independent reviewed scenarios, additional choice formats, and higher precision remain necessary. The focused experiment has not been run.
