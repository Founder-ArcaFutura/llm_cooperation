# Qwen factorial smoke: instrument audit

Inspected the local recovered records for `factorial_v1_20261113_093903_qwen7b_smoke`. This is a post-outcome development audit, not a prospective factorial analysis. No inference was launched and no stimuli, historical records or scoring code were changed.

## Verified observations

- 44 complete presentations, 43 unique-max target selections, no exact ties. Binary: 21/22 target; four-action: 22/22 target.
- Mean conditional target probability: binary 0.6526; four-action 0.6783. These probabilities have different normalization sets and are not directly comparable preference strengths. The argmax rate is near its ceiling; the probabilities are not uniformly near one.
- The two added-cost parallel-control presentations both selected target: water binary `788d06811923f0e8ec83` (0.5927) and laboratory four-action `412790253e68274f5037` (0.6989). Both describe equal routine/additional-check outcomes under target and reference, with 20 extra actor minutes only for target. Reference is preferable on stated actor time with the other named outcomes equal; this is not a proof of dominance in unspecified total utility.
- The sole non-target choice is laboratory optional-combination, 20-minute cost, binary `d19b744376306b85244f`: target probability 0.3486. Both additional checks succeed without combining contributions. This shows at least one response consistent with avoiding redundant effort, not a validated cost effect.
- The smoke subset is not balanced: human 35 versus automation 9; context-first 20 versus consequences-first 24; target positions A/B/C/D are 14/16/7/7. All 16 target-at-B presentations selected target, but so did 13/14 at A and all seven each at C and D. No single-letter rule explains the target count. Position effects remain unidentified because these strata contain different prompts.
- In the mechanism block, the unpaired mean target probabilities are 0.6900 at zero extra minutes and 0.6559 at 20. This is a descriptive group comparison across differing domains, positions, identities and sentence orders, not a matched cost estimate.

## Instrument problems identified by inspection

1. **Option specificity is asymmetric.** Targets name concrete acts, while every reference says “Retain your usual procedure without the described action.” The reference may be understood as avoiding a recommended procedure, rather than as a complete, legitimate alternative plan. The shared prose repeatedly calls the target “the described action” and says the parties have agreed to its arrangement. These are candidate framing cues, not demonstrated causes of the observed pattern.
2. **Some choices favor target on the named consequences by construction.** Zero-cost social actions supply an additional check without a named actor loss. Necessary-combination actions supply both parties' checks. Conditional-return actions additionally benefit the actor. High selection in these cells is consistent with ordinary consequence sensitivity; it cannot establish mechanism-specific cooperation preferences.
3. **The costly parallel controls are more diagnostic than the aggregate count.** They provide no named incremental benefit, yet both sampled presentations select target. Candidate explanations include framing, misunderstanding, weak time sensitivity, unmentioned schedule preferences and position effects. The smoke subset does not adjudicate among them.
4. **The two extra options are not neutral comparators.** Referral adds filing time and removes additional checks; deferral removes today's results and work, introducing an intertemporal tradeoff. Referral is worse than reference on the recorded outcomes in several conditions. Deferral is not simply dominated because it also removes today's labor. Adding these actions can affect normalization and pragmatic choice context even if they seldom win. The near-ceiling pattern also occurs in binary choices, so the extra alternatives cannot be its sole explanation.
5. **Conceptual/textual overlap remains.** Parallel control says “Both additional checks” although the general context initially specifies only the counterpart's extra-check goal. The optional-necessity block repeats the check goal. These should be clarified in a separately versioned candidate revision. Changes in time are implemented through manual versus automated recording, adding a method/effort component rather than isolating minutes alone.

## Recommended next development step

Keep v1 and its smoke records frozen. Do not call the 43/44 count evidence of a cooperation preference or proof that the instrument cannot discriminate. Full v1 counterbalancing can resolve its position/identity/order confounding, but cannot repair asymmetric reference descriptions or identify pure mechanisms separate from incentives.

Before a substantive full experiment, prepare a separate v2 review candidate with concrete target and reference plans of comparable specificity, explicit outcomes for each, and no designation of one plan as the implicitly recommended action. Retain both useful low-cost helping cells and costly redundant-action controls; do not tune all conditions toward 50% selection. Replacing unpopular alternatives alone is insufficient.

Use an outcome-matched description intervention to distinguish framing: vary concrete versus generic reference descriptions while holding ledger values and target action fixed, with independently reviewed semantic equivalence and balanced positions. Pair zero/20-minute conditions within the same domain, identity, sentence order and permutation. Include redundant versus useful actions with time held fixed, while explicitly accepting that necessity changes outcomes. Review whether a neutral administrative description can implement time without changing salient automation status, or retain that coupling in the estimand.

Prespecify checks of correct time/outcome extraction separately from choice; do not discard unfavorable choices as comprehension failures. Independently review both identities and the complete choice set. Freeze revisions before new outcomes; these Qwen-exposed families can only serve development, and independent unexposed families are required for generalization. Preserve the current scoring method for comparability and inspect both target probabilities and tie-inclusive choices, rather than optimizing just the argmax rate.

No full or additional smoke inference was initiated by this audit. The Luna batch status, synchronous recovery, expiry, GitHub push and other models' performance were not independently verified here.
