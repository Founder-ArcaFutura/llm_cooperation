# Qwen 32B classification: completeness and initial quality review

The Modal output reports 304 of 304 scenario versions in 152 packets, with both target wordings per version. JSON-schema validity is not semantic validity. The saved output records the pinned model revision and input hash; the local join checked the packet-file hash and every canonical behavioral-row hash against the existing provenance manifest. It mapped all 608 scenario-wording records to the 11 responsive base-model cohorts, with no unmapped four-option rows. Results are in `provisional_label_join.json` and are descriptive, not adjudicated or confirmatory.

## Material quality issues in a small spot check

This is not a representative error-rate estimate. It establishes that at least some classifications are unsuitable for direct interpretation without review:

* Packet `RV-760143_A`, customs officer and importer: Qwen called the target tariff-avoidance action better for the actor and the reference worse for the recipient. Its second wording explicitly says “the actor benefits by avoiding costs,” although the actor is the customs officer and the importer pays the tariffs. This appears to reverse roles and outcomes; the provided text does not establish a personal officer benefit. Both wording records need correction.
* Packet `RV-624671_A`, pressure-testing: the candidate says combining data streams shortens the overall test, while the source scenario states a reduction from 50 to 30 minutes for both sectors. Qwen rated actor and recipient outcomes equal and reported an opportunity cost. This conflicts with the stated improvement and requires review of the reference text and both wording records.
* Packet `RV-563792_A`, spectrophotometers: Qwen reports `beneficial_not_necessary` and opportunity cost while its evidence cites identical performance. A simultaneous schedule may impose waiting, but a benefit and opportunity cost are not clearly established by that quoted evidence. An independent reviewer should judge the whole text.

The classifier instruction also incorrectly says “two target alternatives ... each has two phrasings,” although the task presents one target in two phrasings and one reference. It always identifies the target group as such; presentation order of the target and alternative block varies, but it is not a full blind randomization of target versus reference identity. This can contribute to confusion and expectancy bias. These issues are limitations of the instrument we wrote, not a reason to delete the records.

## Exploratory joining only

`analyze_qwen_construct_labels.py` joins each scenario, partner variant and original/paraphrase wording to its matching Qwen judgment, and retains a behavioral presentation only when both wording versions have unique forced-choice maxima. It computes COOPERATE-minus-INDEPENDENT margins per model, bank, wording and assigned category. Categories can overlap in templates across partner/wording variants; repeated rotations are not independent observations. There are no inferential tests in this output, and no claim about causal effects or validated constructs should be made from it.

For example, Phase 1 reworded presentations classified as `interdependence=absent` cover six distinct templates in each model's retained rows, with model-specific margins from 6.2 to 94.4 percentage points; the `beneficial_not_necessary` labels span 26 templates and 29.3 to 93.6 points. Some templates appear in both categories, reflecting different judgments across variants. These ranges are a description of Qwen-assigned labels and model-specific responses, not evidence that the labels are correct or that the groups differ causally. Qwen's judgment in at least one symmetric item is questionable, so even these numbers must not be described as reliable interaction effects.

Keep raw records and the join intact. Next, do a blinded human review of a structured sample spanning categories, explicit actor/recipient outcomes, symmetric controls, and potentially contradictory evidence; assess agreement before trusting category-stratified results. Repair the prompt and compare reviewed items before deciding whether a new model run is useful. Preserve both the original and corrected classifications and report disagreements rather than overwrite them. The existing human-review packet permits independent evaluation without behavioral outcomes; blinding does not undo our prior knowledge of aggregate findings.
