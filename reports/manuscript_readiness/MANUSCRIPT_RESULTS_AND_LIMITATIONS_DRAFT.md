# Results and limitations draft — superseded

This earlier V0.1 draft has been retired because its manually copied estimates and provenance were inconsistent with the recomputed clustered analysis. In particular, it cited stale commit `42aaf61`, contained stale confidence intervals, and incorrectly described an earlier AI-assisted review as human review.

The authoritative, fully integrated manuscript is [MANUSCRIPT_V1.md](MANUSCRIPT_V1.md). Its primary and family tables are generated from `cohort_statistical_readout.json`; its post-hoc tests are generated from `posthoc_significance_results.json`. The updated audit tables are in [MANUSCRIPT_READINESS_REPORT.md](MANUSCRIPT_READINESS_REPORT.md), and methods/assumptions for the randomization tests are in [POSTHOC_SIGNIFICANCE_METHODS.md](POSTHOC_SIGNIFICANCE_METHODS.md).

Unresolved scientific work remains: blinded human semantic adjudication of the prepared 304-item queue; reconciliation of Phase 1 bank metadata (marked held out and unexecuted) with existing executed rows; broader calibration eligibility across at least four families; and a formally eligible prospective replication. All new significance tests are post-hoc exploratory inference.
